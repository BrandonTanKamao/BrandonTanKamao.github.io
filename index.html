How I work

Destructive work runs on rails.

1

Snapshot and plan

Read-only pass that writes the current state to disk and says exactly what would change.

2

Canary, then go

Three records first, every field diffed. Then the run, reading back every write before the next. Three failures in a row aborts.

3

Verify and restore

Re-read everything against the plan. A restore path proven to work before the first real write.

4

Document and hand off

Registry, build guides, runbooks and a weekly audit script, so the client never depends on me.

  • A number without a date is not a number every figure carries the day it was read and how it was counted
  • Rulings are closed a decision the client made is written down with who and when, and never re-raised
  • Test residue never touches a count every test record starts with ZZTEST and gets listed separately
  • Say what you could not reach a source I could not open is named, not guessed at

FAQ

The questions I get on the first call.

What kind of work do you take on?

Migrations into GoHighLevel, full sub-account builds, automation and integration work, and rescue jobs where a previous build is half-finished or the data can no longer be trusted. Project-based, contract and part-time retainers. I am not looking for a full-time seat.

What hours do you work?

I am in the Philippines (UTC+8). That is a full overlap with Australia, Singapore and the UK working day, and evening overlap with US mornings. Backup power and redundant internet, so a brownout does not become your problem.

How do you handle risky changes in a live account?

Nothing destructive runs by hand. Every bulk change is a script that plans first, runs on three records, then runs for real while reading back each write, with a proven restore path before the first real write. The client sees the plan before anything moves.

Can you work inside my existing GHL account?

Yes, that is the usual case. I work as a user in your sub-account, take a snapshot of the current state before touching anything, and keep a registry of every workflow I create or change so you can see exactly what happened.

What happens when the project ends?

You get a written handoff: registry of automations, build guides, runbooks in plain language, and a read-only audit script your team can run to confirm the build still works. The goal is that you are never dependent on me. Retainer support is available if you want it.

How do we start?

Book a 30-minute call. Tell me what is broken or what you need built. I will tell you what I would fix first, roughly what it takes, and whether I am the right person for it.
