How I work

Destructive work runs on rails. Every number carries the date it was read.

1

Snapshot and plan

Read-only pass that writes the current state to disk and says exactly what would change.

2

Canary, then go

Three records first, every field diffed. Then the run, reading back every write. Three failures in a row aborts.

3

Verify and restore

Re-read everything against the plan. A restore path proven before the first real write.

4

Document and hand off

Registry, runbooks and a weekly audit script, so the client never depends on me.

FAQ

The questions I get on the first call.

What kind of work do you take on?

Migrations into GoHighLevel, full sub-account builds, automation and integration work, and rescue jobs where a previous build is half-finished or the data can no longer be trusted. Project-based, contract and part-time retainers. Not looking for a full-time seat.

Can you work inside my existing account, with live automations?

Yes, that is the usual case. I snapshot the current state before touching anything, guard the live triggers so an import never fires your onboarding sequence, and keep a registry of every workflow I create or change.

What don't you do?

About two years of GoHighLevel, not a full-stack developer. No production n8n, no Snapshots sold, no Conversation AI or Voice AI bots shipped. Meta Ads is the technical layer, not media buying. Better you know now than in week two.

What happens when the project ends?

You get a written handoff: registry of automations, plain-language runbooks, and a read-only audit script your team can run to confirm the build still works. Retainer support if you want it, never dependence.
